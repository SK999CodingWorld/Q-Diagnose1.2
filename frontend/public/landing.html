Scope stated up front

Data trust, by construction

Every claim below is a property of this codebase you can verify in the API — not badges.

De-identified & public-dataset first

Runs start from two public benchmarks — WDBC (569×30) and a diabetes regression set binarised at its median (442×10). You can evaluate the whole platform without supplying patient data.

Reproducible by design

Each run stores its seeds, full config, and preprocessing scope. Scalers and encoders fit on training folds only, and re-running a stored config reproduces metrics and per-row probabilities exactly.

Not a diagnostic device

Decision-support research only. Nothing is clinically validated, no regulatory clearance is claimed, and the boundary is repeated in the API response, the dashboard, and this footer.

Built toward DPDP-aligned practice

De-identified inputs, dataset content hashing, and validated tabular ingestion. Framed as direction of travel, not certification — the prototype has no authentication layer yet.

Current limitation, stated plainly

environment-specific

In the pinned Qiskit stack on this machine, the QNN forward pass returns different values for identical inputs. The platform therefore refuses to train or score it and records the reason — no fabricated metrics. Quantum support here is QSVC and VQC. Real hardware stays off unless you supply your own IBM Quantum token; the app never silently falls back to a simulator.

How it works

Four steps, one auditable pipeline

Each step is a real endpoint on this instance. Nothing between them is a black box.

01

Upload data

Register a public dataset or a CSV, get schema, class balance, and a content hash back.

POST /api/datasets/upload
02

Classical + quantum training

Same splits, same preprocessing scope. Logistic, SVM, random forest, XGBoost, QSVC, VQC.

POST /api/experiments
03

Benchmark & explain

Repeated stratified CV against baselines, plus SHAP and permutation attribution per model.

GET /api/experiments/{experiment_id}/explanation
04

Risk score & calibration

Reliability curves, Brier score, and a threshold you can inspect before trusting a score.

GET /api/experiments/{experiment_id}/calibration
